FAQ

Quick answers

Where are you?

300 Alamo Plaza, San Antonio, TX. See the map on Visit.

How do I reach support?

Use the Contact form or email support@hallmarkproject.com. We reply during open hours.

How do I book?

Send a message on Contact, email bookings@hallmarkproject.com, or call +1 (210) 555-0148.

Are prices current?

Listed prices are current and may shift with seasons — we update this site when they do.

Do you take groups?

Yes for many bookings — tell us the size and date and we'll confirm what Hallmarkproject can host.

What should I bring?

Just yourself (and any notes on allergies, sizes, or preferences). We'll handle the rest.

Where do I park?

See the Visit page for address and map links around San Antonio.

Do you update treatments often?

Yes — seasonal shifts are posted here first.

Can I book for someone else?

Yes — include their name and preferences in the Contact form or email support@hallmarkproject.com.
